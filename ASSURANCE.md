# Assurance boundaries

The central theorem is a written analytic candidate, internally checked by coordinated agents. The all-order coefficient, regularity for 1<p<2, rotational kernel and covariance transfer received a targeted analytic audit. This is not formal verification or external peer review.

Code supplies exact finite quadratic algebra and ordinary floating-point diagnostics. Separate implementations are not equivalent to independent external reproduction. Saved records include unsuccessful optimization exits. Numerical-domain exceptions deliberately expose unsupported valid frames; they do not disprove the mathematical formulas.

No global conjecture resolution, certified numerical neighbourhood radius, exhaustive parameter search, interval enclosure, p≤1 extension or exhaustive priority claim is made. Execution receipts prove only the recorded scope and byte binding. [LIMITATIONS.md](LIMITATIONS.md) and [REPLAY_RECEIPT.md](REPLAY_RECEIPT.md) retain detailed qualifications.
