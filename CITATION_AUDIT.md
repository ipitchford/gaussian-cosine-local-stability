# Bounded source comparison

Checked 18 October 2026. This is a bounded statement/object/method comparison, not exhaustive historical priority clearance.

- Dmitriy Kunisky, *A revision of Litvak's conjecture on Gaussian minima and a volumetric zone conjecture*, arXiv:2605.02023v1. Proposition 2.1 gives the cosine distribution; Theorem 2.2 gives its moments. Conjecture 3.1 is global/stochastic and stronger than the present local result. The conditional zone comparison does not close it. https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02023v1
- Y. Long, *Hyperbolic embedding of infinite-dimensional convex bodies*, arXiv:2305.13428v3, DOI 10.1007/s10711-024-00957-5. Theorem 1.2, equation (1.1), specializes to the unit-vertex maximum identity. It is a dependency, not a new result here. Older Chevet/Schneider antecedents mentioned there were not independently inspected in full. https://arxiv.org/html/2305.13428v3
- cyclemath, *Convex Hulls of Great Subspheres and Gaussian Minima*, preliminary source dated 16 September 2026. Its object is a hull of central disks rather than the finite signed-vertex hull; the nonnegative correction does not supply the missing reverse inequality. Its conclusion leaves the conjectures open. Source statements and cosine section inspected; no external-peer-review claim. https://github.com/cyclemath/great-subspheres-gaussian-minima

Targeted searches combined Gaussian minima with Hessian, second variation, cosine, local minimality and moments. No exact antecedent for the all-n, p>1 transverse local theorem was located in the consulted sources. Search recall and source coverage are limited; this is not a first-ever claim. The global conjecture was not resolved by the sources inspected or by this package.
